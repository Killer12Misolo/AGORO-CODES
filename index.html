THE ARTIST

The story behind the music.

Agoro Likwenyo is a Kenyan Afro-fusion artist, singer-songwriter, and live performer whose music blends Afro-soul, R&B, jazz, and contemporary African rhythms into rich, emotionally resonant experiences. Known for his warm stage presence and expressive storytelling, he creates music that celebrates love, identity, community, and the beauty of everyday life.

As the frontman of The City Band, Agoro delivers immersive live performances that combine original compositions with carefully selected reinterpretations of timeless classics. His performances are characterized by soulful vocals, dynamic musicianship, and meaningful audience engagement, making each show both intimate and memorable.

His debut album, Certified Loverman, showcases his signature blend of Afro-fusion, R&B, and jazz influences, reflecting his dedication to honest songwriting and musical craftsmanship. Whether performing at festivals, corporate events, intimate concerts, or cultural showcases, Agoro Likwenyo continues to inspire audiences through music that connects people across generations and backgrounds.
